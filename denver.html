About Denver

Denver, the capital city of Colorado, is known as the Mile High City for its elevation of 5,280 feet. It's the state's economic and cultural hub.

Demographics

Diverse population with rapid growth in recent decades.

Average Salary

The average salary in Denver ($92,504) is slightly lower than state average ($97,113), supported by tech, finance, and healthcare jobs.

Cost of Living

The cost of living is relatively high, especially due to housing demand.

Weather

Four distinct seasons with dry, sunny days and snowy winters.

Highlight: Major sports teams like Broncos and Nuggets
Highlight: Gateway to the Rocky Mountains
