Fort Collins Quick Facts

  • Population: 170,000 (approx.)
  • Incorporated: 1864
  • Region: Northern Colorado, near the Cache la Poudre River
  • Classification: Urban/Suburban
  • Average Income vs State: $81,199 vs $97,113

About Fort Collins

Fort Collins is a vibrant college town, home to Colorado State University, and is known for its craft beer and historic Old Town.

Demographics

Fort Collins has a large student population and a diverse mix of familes, professionals, and retirees.

Average Salary

Average salaries in Fort Collins ($81,199) are lower than the state average ($97,113), with jobs in education, brewing, and tech sectors.

Cost of Living

Cost of living is moderately high, though generally lower than Boulder or Denver

Weather

Fort Collins enjoys four distinct seasons, with warm summers and snowy winters, similar to Denver but slightly colder.
