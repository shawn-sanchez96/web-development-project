Boulder Quick Facts

  • Population: 104,000 (approx.)
  • Incorporated: 1871
  • Region: Northern Front Range, foothills of the Rockies
  • Classification: Urban/Suburban
  • Average Income vs State: $102,697 vs $97,113

About Boulder

Boulder is known for its stunning Flatiron rock formations, progressive culture, and the University of Colorado Boulder campus.

Demographics

Boulder has a younger population due to the university, with a strong presence of professionals in tech and research.

Average Salary

Boulder residents have some of the highest average incomes in the state, $102,697, driven by the technology and education sectors. This is slightly higher than the state average of $97,113.

Cost of Living

Cost of living is very high, especially housing, ranking among the most expensive areas in Colorado.

Weather

Boulder experiences sunny days most of the year with snowy winters and moderate summers.
